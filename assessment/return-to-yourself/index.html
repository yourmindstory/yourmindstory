Why Cognitive Behavioral Hypnotherapy?

Knowing how you want to respond and being able to respond that way when you're overwhelmed are two different things.

Hypnosis uses focused attention and suggestion. Rather than giving you more information about him to think about, the sessions focus on the thoughts, feelings and responses we're actually working with.

So if we're working on Break the Pull, for example, I'm not going to spend the session explaining why you shouldn't call him. You already know that. We're working with what happens when you know all of that and the impulse to pick up the phone comes anyway.

If we're working on Restore Self-Trust, we're not simply telling you to “have better boundaries.” We're working with what happens when you've already made the boundary and then the discomfort comes.

And if we're working on Return to Yourself, we're not telling you to “focus on yourself.” We're working on the thoughts, feelings and responses that keep pulling your attention away from your own life.

Hypnosis has been studied scientifically for decades, including research into attention, perception, cognition and behaviour. Scientists continue to investigate the mechanisms involved, so I am not going to tell you we're “rewiring your brain” or make promises the research can't support.
