Questions women ask before they start

What actually is Cognitive Behavioural Hypnotherapy?

It combines cognitive and behavioural therapeutic techniques with hypnosis. In this work, that means looking at thoughts and interpretations, practising different responses, and using focused attention, imagery and therapeutic suggestion as part of that practice.

What if I've never tried hypnotherapy?

You do not need prior experience. You are not asleep or handing control of your mind to somebody else. You participate in the process.

What if I still love him or want the relationship?

You do not have to be over him to start working on yourself. Working on your response is not a verdict on the relationship.

Are you going to tell me to leave or go no contact?

No. I do not make that relationship decision for you. The work focuses on what the situation is doing to you and the choices you want to be able to make for yourself.

I already understand avoidant attachment. How is this different?

Because understanding is not necessarily the part you are struggling with anymore. The work is aimed at the response that can still happen despite what you know.

How do I know where to start?

If one stage is obviously loud, you can buy it directly. If you are unsure, the free 15-question assessment gives you a personalised starting recommendation.
