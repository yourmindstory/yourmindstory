## Core sales argument

### Recognition

You understand the cycle. But you are still stuck in what it is doing to you.

You may understand why he pulls away. You may know exactly what you should do.

And you are still checking.
Still replaying.
Still waiting.
Still reaching out.
Still asking what his behaviour means, whether he misses you, whether he will come back, whether the silence means it is over, whether he ever really loved you, or what you should do next.
Still changing your mind and second-guessing yourself.
Still playing the whole thing over in your head when what you really want is some peace.

**Understanding the cycle is not the same as breaking it.**

### Diagnosis

Knowing what you should do and being able to do it when you are triggered are not the same thing.

You probably do not need more information about him. You have watched the videos. Read the posts. Learned about avoidant attachment.

More information can help you understand why this is happening. But information alone does not automatically change what happens in you when it happens again.

The question needs to move from:

**Why is he doing this?**

to:

**What has this started doing to me?**

The Bootcamp works on the part you actually have access to: your alarm response, the pull to check/reach/replay, the loss of self-trust, and the way the situation starts taking over your attention and life.

## Four-stage transformation

### Step 1 — Quiet the Alarm

A message, a silence, a thought or a memory does not have to send your whole system into alert. You can think, sleep and get through your day without everything suddenly feeling like an emergency.

### Step 2 — Break the Pull

You can feel the urge without automatically checking, reaching out or going back. The feeling can be there without making the decision for you.

### Step 3 — Restore Self-Trust

You start believing yourself again. You can make a decision, hold a boundary and hear your own judgement without having to reopen everything the moment your feelings change or he comes back.

### Step 4 — Return to Yourself

Your attention starts belonging to your own life again. You can make plans, be present with people you love and have mental space that is not occupied by him.

## What “take your life back” means in real life

It does not mean never thinking about him again.
It does not mean stopping loving him on command.
It does not mean becoming a woman who needs nobody.

It can look like:

- He goes quiet and your entire day does not disappear with him.
- You see him online and do not need to investigate what it means.
- You feel the urge to check and do not automatically act on it.
- You miss him without abandoning yourself.
- He comes back and the relief does not have to make the decision for you.
- You set a boundary and do not spend the rest of the evening wondering whether you were unreasonable.
- You make a decision and do not have to remake it every time your feelings change.
- You are with somebody you love and you are actually there.
- You stop needing to know what he is thinking before you are allowed to have peace.

**You become someone you can trust.**

## Objections — approved answers

### What if I’m still with him?

You can still do the Bootcamp. You do not have to stop loving somebody before you can stop losing yourself in the relationship.

### What if it’s already over?

Ending the relationship and ending what is happening inside you are not always the same thing. If you are still checking, replaying, waiting, hoping, questioning yourself or spending huge amounts of time thinking about him, there is still something here to work on.

### Are you going to tell me to go no contact?

No. This programme is not here to make your relationship decisions for you. It is here to help you get steadier, clearer and more connected to yourself while you make them.

### I already know loads about avoidant attachment. Is this going to tell me everything I already know?

No. If information alone were enough, you probably would not still be fighting the same internal battle. This is not the next piece of information. **It is the next piece of work.**

### I’ve already tried therapy. Why would this be different?

This is not a claim that therapy failed or that the Bootcamp replaces therapy. This programme is deliberately focused on this specific pattern and on practising different responses through guided Cognitive Behavioural Hypnotherapy, weekly reflection and progress check-ins. The question is not whether you have understood yourself before. It is whether this particular pattern is still running your thoughts, reactions and choices now.

### What if I’m the kind of person who buys a course and then doesn’t finish it?

That is exactly why this is not built like a heavy online course. Your main daily job is to press play. Once a week you reflect and check in. Every three weeks we look at what is changing. If you miss a day or have a bad week, the instruction is not “you failed.” It is **return.**

### What if I check, contact him or get pulled back in during the 12 weeks?

That does not automatically mean the work is not working and it does not mean you have failed the programme. Use what happened as information. Notice it, check in, return to the work and keep building the response you want to have more often.

### I don’t have loads of spare time. How much am I actually being asked to do?

Your main daily job is the guided work. Once a week, allow around 15 minutes for your Your Mind Story reflection and short check-in. The group integration calls happen every three weeks. This is deliberately not built around weekly teaching videos, piles of worksheets or daily journalling.

### Why wouldn’t I just buy one of the £37 audios?

If one part of the cycle is clearly the problem, a focused audio may be enough to start. The Bootcamp is for when several parts are feeding each other, or when you want to work through the whole pattern in order: the alarm, the pull, your self-trust and getting your attention and life back to you.

### What if I’ve been stuck in this for years?

You do not have to be newly out of the relationship for this to be relevant. Start from where you are now and track movement against your own baseline. The aim is not to pretend years disappear overnight. It is to work on the reactions and patterns that are still active in your life now.

### What if I miss one of the live calls?

A missed call does not erase the work you have already done. The core work continues through your current guided step, weekly reflection and check-in. The calls are integration points in the programme, not the only place the work happens. Return to your current step and keep going.

## Risk-reversal copy

### What if I genuinely do the work and nothing changes?

We are not going to wait until Week 12 before we look.

That is what the check-ins and three-week reviews are for.

You do not have to be perfect. But you do have to participate.

That means using the guided work consistently, completing your weekly Your Mind Story reflections and doing your check-ins.

If you genuinely complete the core 12 weeks and, at the end, you cannot identify meaningful movement in the areas we have been tracking, I will personally review your progress with you.

And if, after that review, neither of us can identify meaningful movement from where you started, **I will refund what you paid for the Bootcamp.**

If you have genuinely participated, I am not going to leave you at Week 12 with: “Well, you tried.”
