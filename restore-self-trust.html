Your Mind Story

YOUR RESULT

Restore Self Trust

You know something doesn't feel right. Then you start talking yourself out of what you know.

You question what you saw, what you felt and whether you were right to feel that way. A decision that felt clear can suddenly become something you second-guess.

That is the strongest part of the cycle showing up in your answers.

Trust what you know.

The goal isn't to be certain about everything.

It's to stop doubting yourself out of the things you already know matter to you.

So when something doesn't feel right, or you decide what you need, you can trust yourself enough to stand by it.
