Immediately. Your guided sessions are delivered digitally after purchase, so you can start feeling relief today.

No. The programme is designed to be completed in stages. Being stuck in an avoidant relationship cycle is draining enough already. This is designed to reduce your mental load, not add to it. Start with your first session, listen as instructed and complete your weekly check-ins.

What if I have already tried everything?

You've probably spent months researching, journalling, analysing and trying to talk yourself out of how you feel. You might even have been to therapy. But you still cannot seem to switch off the loop of him playing in your mind. This isn't traditional talking therapy or another collection of information. No. You can still love him, miss him or want the relationship. You might still be together, on and off, separated or unsure what happens next. I'm not here to tell you whether to stay or leave. You don't have to make a decision about him before you start working on what this is doing to you.

No. These are guided Cognitive Behavioural Hypnotherapy sessions you can listen to in your own time, with weekly check-in support to help you track what's changing. Over 1,800 women have taken the Your Mind Story assessment, helping us understand the patterns women experience in avoidant relationship dynamics. The sessions are built around four recurring areas identified through Your Mind Story's work with women: anxiety; compulsive checking and overthinking; self-doubt; losing yourself. This isn't generic detachment advice or a generic detachment hypnotherapy recording. It's a four-stage programme specifically designed around the patterns women describe when they're caught in an avoidant relationship cycle.

Some women have reported feeling calmer after their first few listens. Everyone responds differently, and no particular result is guaranteed. The sessions are designed to be used consistently.
