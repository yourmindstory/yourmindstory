This is not about him. It never was.

I am Onye. Clinical Cognitive-Behavioural Hypnotherapist. I did not come to this through a textbook. I came to this because I lived it. I went through an avoidant dynamic myself. I know what it is to understand the pattern completely and still not be able to stop the loop. I know what it is to check before you have decided to. To replay the same conversation for the fourth time in a day. To look completely fine to everyone around you and feel completely hollowed out underneath. The only reason I got out sooner than most is because I had the tools. I knew how to work with what was happening in my nervous system. So I built those tools specifically for this. I trained in Cognitive-Behavioural Hypnotherapy because I could see that one modality on its own was not enough. CBT reaches the thoughts. Hypnotherapy reaches underneath them. Combined, they can reach where this actually lives. After my own experience, I went back to my training and built a framework specifically for the nervous system impact of avoidant relationship dynamics. Six areas. Four audios. Tools that do not ask you to understand him. Tools that work on what the dynamic has done to you. Everyone else in this space is talking about him. His patterns. His behaviour. His diagnosis. I talk about what that dynamic has done to your nervous system. And what it actually takes to interrupt the loop. I came to TikTok to share what I knew. The account grew because women were tired of being told to understand him better. They already understood. They needed something that could reach where this actually lives. 12 million views later, the account is called @dontsleepwithavoidantmen. The brand is called Your Mind Story. Because this was never about him. It is about the story you are living in your own mind, and what happens when you change it.
