Privacy and Disclaimer

Who we are

Your Mind Story is operated by Onye Iweha, Clinical Cognitive-Behavioural Hypnotherapist. Contact: via TikTok @dontsleepwithavoidantmen or through payhip.com/YourMindStory.

What data we collect

When you complete the assessment we collect your name and email address, your responses to the assessment questions, and your assessment result. This data is stored securely in Google Sheets and used to send your results and follow-up support emails.

How we use your data

Your email address is added to our MailerLite mailing list only if you tick the consent box during the assessment. We use your data to send your personalised results, occasional support emails relevant to your result, and updates about new resources. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties except as required to operate our email service (MailerLite) and payment processing (Payhip).

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent at any time, and object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us via TikTok or Payhip. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. We use session storage to carry your assessment result to the results page. This data is not stored beyond your browser session.

Disclaimer

Your Mind Story materials and sessions are delivered by a qualified Clinical Cognitive-Behavioural Hypnotherapist. All materials are for educational and personal development purposes only and are not a substitute for medical diagnosis, psychiatric care, or crisis intervention.

These materials do not constitute therapy and do not replace professional mental health support. Results vary. Do not listen to audio materials while driving or operating machinery.

If you are in immediate distress or at risk of harm, please contact emergency services or your GP immediately. In the UK, you can also contact Samaritans on 116 123 (free, 24 hours).

By using these materials you confirm you are 18 or over and accept full responsibility for your own wellbeing.

Products and refunds

Due to the nature of immediate digital access, all sales are final. If you experience a technical issue preventing access to your purchase, contact us and we will resolve it.
